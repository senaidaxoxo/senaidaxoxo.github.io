# Goals

## The north star: financial freedom by 30

The most concrete long-term goal, stated explicitly: financial freedom by age 30. SENAIDA is 23 as of May 2026, which means 7 years to hit this target. The Life Audit is direct about the stakes: *"the decisions made in the next 90 days will determine whether that is achievable or whether they remain in a holding pattern of high-visibility, low-stability creative life."*

This goal is currently at risk. The financial picture as of May 2026 shows income that is diversified but not yet sufficient for independence: ~$3,659 in artist income (Jan–May 2026), $500/month Cyberian stipend, and a handful of smaller streams (Patreon, AdSense, speaker fees). Financial dependence on Ricky, parents, and social goodwill is the present reality.

## Five-year personal vision (with Ricky)

Move to Goa or Thailand, build a house together, raise a child, then move to China to make art, invest, and continue traveling the world. This is a deeply articulated shared vision with Ricky Kapoor — not an abstract aspiration but a specific plan they are actively building toward. It is the life goal most tied to a relationship rather than a career.

## 90-day execution priorities (from Life Audit, May 2026)

Three high-leverage moves identified, in priority order:

**1. Rebuild private creative practice.** The single highest-priority action: sit at the piano for 20 minutes with no goal, no recording, no audience. This is the root system of everything else — creative confidence, authentic content, long-term DJ identity, and the psychological foundation for harder decisions about Flutter all depend on it. The target is 4 private music-making sessions completed in 90 days (with no output required).

**2. Reposition Flutter/Cyberian as a neurofeedback + music wellness platform.** Rewrite the $40k friends-and-family pitch to lead with the neurofeedback vision rather than the couples haptics app. Flutter becomes the consumer entry point, not the whole company. This unlocks a wealthier funding pool (wellness/longevity investors), EU health-tech grants, and a product category with genuine defensibility that matches SENAIDA's actual rare skillset. Send the fundraising video only *after* this reframe is complete.

**3. Monetize artist-entrepreneur knowledge immediately.** Create one paid offer this week (target: €97 "Independent Artist Toolkit" — contacts list + resource guide, posted once on Instagram). Target: €500+ from knowledge products in a single month within 90 days, independent of DJing. This solves the immediate financial problem without compromising artistic identity.

Additional 90-day milestones: friends-and-family raise complete or formally closed; Flutter retention data gathered from first 45 users; honest co-founder survival conversation with Jason; decision made on Berlin visa (yes or no, with timeline).

**International touring:** The 2026 schedule is robust — NXNE (Toronto, June), Milan (July), Berlin (July), Prague (August), The Hague and Istanbul (September). Upcoming travel also includes Madrid, NYC, and European day trips. The goal of international presence across 25+ countries is already a lived reality; deepening and sustaining it is the ongoing work.

**KUNST KAPUTT continuation:** Move from debut to sustained presence — the global tour (Berlin, Hong Kong, Milan) and ongoing blockchain revenue model create a foundation worth developing.

## Long-term (structural ambitions)

**WE ARE THE FUTURE! as lasting infrastructure.** SENAIDA names this their proudest project because of its direct impact. The long-term goal is building WATF! from NYC event series to something with more permanent reach — a label and collective with enduring influence on underground music culture.

**Structural change in music technology.** The cyberfeminist framework, KUNST KAPUTT's revenue model, the academic research — together these point toward a goal larger than personal success: changing who gets to make music, who gets paid, and who the technology is designed for.

**Research-practice bridge.** The Cyborg Synchrony paper and Cyberian Technologies sit at the edge of academic research and commercial product. Building work that lives credibly in both — peer-reviewed and practice-tested — is a sustained long-term goal.

## The stop-doing list

From the audit: stop sending the fundraising video with current positioning before reframing it; stop reaching out to UGC creators without retention data; stop giving away the contacts list and creative entrepreneurship knowledge for free; stop making every creative act into content; stop avoiding the financial conversation by calling it a values position; stop treating the Jason co-founder dynamic as stable without having the direct conversation.

## Tensions

The breadth of simultaneous ambitions — touring, startup, collective, research, private creative practice — creates real resource tensions. The audit makes the forced-choice explicit if implicitly: the private creative practice is the most urgent priority, because everything else runs downstream from it.
