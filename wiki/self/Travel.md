# Travel

Travel is not incidental to SENAIDA's life — it is the structural condition of it. The last 18 months represent a near-continuous movement across four continents, with DJ gigs, art events, and community residencies stitching together the route. This page documents the travel record as its own entity, distinct from the [[Work]] page's focus on what happened at each stop.

## Reconstructed Route: Late 2025 – Present

### India (late 2025 – Jan 2026)
Dharamkot (Himachal Pradesh) was the base for a stretch of DJ work at EDM Hostel around New Year's Eve 2025/2026. Payments recorded in INR, routed through a local contact ("Ricky") who appears repeatedly as an India tour liaison.

### Southeast Asia (Feb 2026)
**Feb 2:** Chennai → Bangkok (Thai Lion Air SL241). The India chapter closed and Southeast Asia opened.  
**Feb 8:** Koh Phangan → Bangkok (Sapthaweephol bus/ferry — from the ferry pier at Thong Sala). A side trip to Koh Phangan island before returning to Bangkok proper.  
Bangkok served as the transit hub. **Feb 9** marks a Diffusion Taipei online gig — likely a recorded or remote set, not physical travel.

### Hong Kong & China (Feb–Mar 2026)
From Bangkok, the route moved north into Hong Kong and then deep into mainland China.  
**Mar 8:** ABYSS x UMAMI gig in Hong Kong (paid HKD).  
**Mar 6:** HK → Guangzhou (High-speed train, G6556).  
The China chapter then traced a path through Yunnan and up to Beijing — an unusual touring route that suggests artistic interest in non-obvious China stops:  
- **Lijiang** (Yunnan)  
- **Dali** (Yunnan): Two gigs on Mar 13 — Pools Dali and TungDin

Of note: Hakka Bar in Chengdu, given the centrality of Hakka identity to SENAIDA's practice, feels like more than a coincidental booking.

## Patterns in movement

The routing is telling. Rather than the standard European club circuit, the 2025-2026 Asia tour went deep into China's inland cities (Dali, Chengdu, Lijiang, Hangzhou), through South and Southeast Asia, and across India's less-obvious DJ markets (Dharamkot, Bhubaneswar, Goa). This is a routing shaped by artistic curiosity and community relationships as much as booking fees.

The recurring India contact "Ricky" appears across multiple gigs in Dharamkot and Bhubaneswar — suggesting a sustained relationship with the Indian underground that may deepen over time.

Transport choices are also revealing: trains through China (not flights), a bus ferry from Koh Phangan, a FlixBus from Toronto to NYC. This is not the travel budget of a well-funded operation — it's resourceful, overland-first where possible.
