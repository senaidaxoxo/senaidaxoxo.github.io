# Relationships

## Ricky Kapoor — Partner

Ricky Kapoor is SENAIDA's partner of four years (together since May 5, 2022). He lives in Chennai, India, where he works at Derby Tailor Studio for his father. SENAIDA spent four months living with Ricky and his family in Chennai in early 2026 — a significant sustained period of domestic life together that is otherwise rare given the touring lifestyle.

The relationship has weathered extraordinary circumstances. Ricky was present through SENAIDA's lowest moment — when they were scammed out of $11,000 and considered jumping off the Williamsburg Bridge — and through the highs of the DJ career taking off. He was there when SENAIDA started DJing and has been a witness to the full arc of the career.

Their shared five-year vision: move to Goa or Thailand, build a house together, raise a child, and eventually move to China to make art and invest. They bond over music and food. The relationship carries unusual weight as the most consistent intimate anchor in a life defined by movement.

**India gig facilitation:** In the budget records, multiple India DJ gigs (Dharamkot, BudxLoft, Bhubaneswar) appear as "via Ricky" or "Cash to Ricky" — he functions as an informal local tour liaison in addition to being her partner, likely drawing on local contacts and managing on-the-ground logistics for Indian appearances.

**The financial dimension:** The Life Audit notes that Ricky is "carrying more financial weight than is fair" — SENAIDA has relied on Ricky as part of a broader pattern of financial dependence (partner, parents, social goodwill) while avoiding direct confrontation of the income problem. This is a dynamic worth watching carefully as it puts asymmetric strain on the relationship.

## Jason Snell — Co-founder & Roommate

Jason is the most visible professional relationship and also SENAIDA's current roommate in Berlin. They co-authored the Cyborg Synchrony paper (*Frontiers in Computer Science*, 2025), co-founded Cyberian Technologies (where SENAIDA is CEO), and are building Flutter (the Apple Watch app) together.

Living together adds a dimension of intensity to the co-founder dynamic that the Life Audit flags explicitly: the audit recommends having a "survival conversation" about Flutter's 60-day reality before a "vision alignment exercise" — signaling that the co-founder relationship may carry some tension between shared optimism and unspoken practical stress. Separating the working relationship from the living situation would likely be difficult.

## Parents

SENAIDA's parents are alive and healthy, living in Thornhill, Ontario (where SENAIDA grew up). Their father runs an IT consulting company that he is trying to sell — the business is struggling and he is managing an exit. Their mother works in downtown Toronto in prenatal immigration services. The family appears stable and not estranged, though the distance between Berlin and Toronto, and the nature of the touring life, means day-to-day contact is limited.

The family context matters for the financial picture: SENAIDA's Life Audit notes financial dependence on parents (alongside Ricky and "social goodwill") as part of the structural problem they need to solve.

## Relationship structure

The relationship with Ricky is open. SENAIDA writes explicitly: "Ricky allowing me to be free and fully explore in all aspects of my life. He is my anchor." The structure appears mutually understood and consensual. During the Berlin and Goa periods (Aug 2025 – Jan 2026), SENAIDA maintained meaningful intimate connections with several people alongside the primary partnership with Ricky.

**RADAR check-ins:** SENAIDA and Ricky use a structured communication method called RADAR for relationship check-ins — used notably in Chennai (Nov 2025) and on the boat after MO:DEM (Feb 2026). The method creates a formal container for honesty about feelings and needs. "We both found more clarity and support through doing the exercise."

## Community as relational substrate

SENAIDA's most structurally important relationships are embedded in communities they've built. WE ARE THE FUTURE! creates direct artist-to-artist connections; the GEM Club (300+ members in its first year) was designed as a relational infrastructure as much as an advocacy organization. The network of radio stations (Refuge Worldwide, Operator Radio, No Signal, Baihui, AAJA, HKCR, Snatchers, Air Berlin Alexanderplatz) suggests ongoing relationships with programmers and curators across Berlin, London, and globally.

SENAIDA describes having many friends in Berlin and around the world, and loving them deeply — while also acknowledging that constant movement means never spending enough time with them. The longing for sustained presence with people they love runs against the structural conditions of touring life. This is an acknowledged tension, not a resolved one.
