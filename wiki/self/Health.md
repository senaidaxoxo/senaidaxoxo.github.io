# Health

## Physical baseline

SENAIDA is 5'3" and 103 lbs. Biometric data suggests generally good cardiovascular health: resting heart rate of 66 bpm (healthy range) and HRV of 28ms. The HRV figure warrants attention — HRV of 28ms is on the lower end of the healthy range for someone their age and active lifestyle, suggesting there may be some chronic stress or recovery deficit present despite overall good health. They describe themselves as "in good health."

## Morning routine

The daily anchor is a structured morning sequence: meditation, breathwork, yoga, squats, morning skincare routine, making coffee and breakfast, and smoking a cigarette. This is an unusually deliberate morning practice for someone with a touring lifestyle — it suggests an understanding of routine as stabilizing infrastructure. The cigarette at the end is a notable contradiction in an otherwise health-oriented routine.

## Diet

Vegetarian, with the exception of sushi. This applies regardless of country — a constraint that requires active management while touring through China, India, and Southeast Asia, where the vegetarian landscape varies enormously.

## Mental health

The most significant documented mental health event: SENAIDA was scammed out of $11,000 and, in the aftermath, stood on the Williamsburg Bridge considering jumping. This is a serious crisis point — one that Ricky was present for and that represents the lowest documented low in their life. The incident predates the current Berlin period and appears to have been survived and integrated rather than suppressed (they mentioned it directly). Whether it has been processed through therapy or other support is not known.

The Life Audit identifies a current psychological pattern with mental health implications: the widening gap between performed identity (internationally touring artist-researcher-entrepreneur) and actual practice (no music made in 18 months, piano untouched in six years) creates a form of chronic inauthenticity that the audit describes as corrosive to creative capacity. This isn't a clinical diagnosis but a functional observation about the cost of avoidance over time.

## Energy and touring demands

Touring at the pace documented — 11+ international flights in 5 months across four continents, with DJ sets in the same cities — is physically demanding. The morning routine serves as a stabilizer, but the structural demands of irregular schedules, time zones, and late-night sets make baseline recovery difficult to maintain.

The audit notes SENAIDA is "more burnt out on social media than they are now" as a projected state if nothing changes — indicating that some degree of digital/content burnout is already present.

## Back and shoulder issues

Back and shoulder tension is a recurring physical issue. Journal entries reference energy "crystallized in the shoulders and neck." In January 2026, Ricky's mother took SENAIDA to physiotherapy in Chennai, which provided significant relief. The issue is likely exacerbated by the amount of time spent at a laptop, irregular postures while traveling, and the physical demands of performing. A posture brace has been mentioned as a corrective tool.

## Sleep patterns

A chronic night owl. Left to natural rhythm, SENAIDA stays up until morning and sleeps until midday or later — "my sleep schedule was extremely off" (Aug 2025). The morning routine serves as an anchor to regulate this, but travel, late-night DJ sets, and festival periods consistently disrupt it. Oversleeping creates a subsequent mood dip: "I couldn't find the motivation to get out of bed. I would still feel tired after waking up."

## Psychedelics and altered states

Psychedelics appear as an intentional practice rather than recreational use. Shrooms were taken with Ricky at Bhujal waterfall (Dharamshala, Nov 2025) and in Goa (Dec 2025). Acid at MO:DEM festival (Koh Phangan, Feb 2026) with Ricky — a difficult trip navigated together that brought them closer. Each use is described in the context of nature, ceremony, and intentional presence. SENAIDA has noted they no longer enjoy cannabis ("I don't like smoking weed anymore," Nov 2025).

## Appetite and menstrual health

A period of severely diminished appetite was noted in December 2025 (during the Goa/India travel period). Menstrual cramps are described as severe enough to cause physical weakness and difficulty being around people. These are not elaborated on but worth tracking as potential health signals.

## Berlin ambivalence and physical environment

SENAIDA moved to Berlin for the DJ career and sound healing community, has a "nice room" and feels "relatively free," but doesn't love Berlin — specifically noting there are "too many white people." The physical and social environment of where one lives has direct effects on daily wellbeing; the ambivalence about Berlin suggests the living situation is tolerated rather than chosen.

## Lifestyle areas of focus

Several health-adjacent areas SENAIDA wants to develop: optimizing sleep and wake time, structuring a more consistent workout practice, and maintaining the morning routine while traveling. These are aspirational rather than established.

## Body and technology

One dimension of health that appears deliberately in the research: the biofeedback work (MiSynth, Cyborg Synchrony, live EEG performance with heartbeat as kick drum) positions the body as both instrument and data source. This suggests a particular kind of embodied self-awareness — though whether this sophisticated bodily intelligence extends consistently into everyday self-care is an open question.
