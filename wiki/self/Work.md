# Work

SENAIDA's output spans at least five distinct but interconnected domains: DJ and producer practice, visual and installation art, academic research, community and label-building, and entrepreneurship. These are not separate career tracks — they cross-pollinate continuously, with research informing artistic work, club practice informing theory, and community work creating the context for everything else.

## DJ / Producer practice

SENAIDA transitioned from songwriter-producer to DJ in 2022 after studying abroad in Berlin. Their debut DJ set was on Refuge Worldwide in December 2022 — immediately at a serious radio platform, not a house party. They subsequently built residencies at XPIZZA (NYC, until its closure in 2024) and Groundless Factory (Beijing), and guest mixed on Refuge Worldwide, Operator Radio, No Signal, Baihui, Snatchers, Air Berlin Alexanderplatz, AAJA, and HKCR.

Their sets are described as bold, high-energy, genre-defying — techno-adjacent but drawing from a wide range of influences, informed by a classical pianist's ear for dynamics and phrasing. The Resident Advisor profile uses "shaman of the dancefloor." A ShoutOut LA quote from SENAIDA: *"I see DJing as an extension of expressing a moment's essence, with clubbing as a sacred ritual."*

They have toured in 25+ countries across four continents: USA, Canada, Germany, Netherlands, Greece, China, Colombia, France, Belgium, Thailand, India, and more. Key international venues include OXI (Berlin), EXIT (Shanghai), Temple (Athens), Şahika (Istanbul). In NYC: 99 Scott, Bossa Nova Civic Club, Silence Please.

**Upcoming (2026):** NXNE Music Festival (Toronto, June), Oriental Techno Club (Milan, July), Shakti's Voyage to Venus (Berlin, July), HEAT Pride Party (Prague, August), Club Hop with Royal Surf Club (The Hague, September), NOH Radio + BANGER with Royal Surf Club (Istanbul, September).

They also host **Zeroes + Ones**, a radio show on WNYU 89.1 FM and Subtle Radio.

## Albums & releases

**KUNST KAPUTT** (2024) is the debut album, described as "the world's first participatory generative AI album." Released December 13, 2024 in New York alongside an immersive exhibition of 10 new media artworks (250+ attendees). The album critiques the exploitative streaming economy: listeners can use AI to remix tracks, upload to the blockchain, and split revenue 50-50 with SENAIDA. The project was a finalist for the 2025 AI Creative Futures Award (AICA) in Tokyo and was featured in Mixmag Asia. Paper Magazine described the track *Orbital XVI* as conjuring "futuristic dystopia reminiscent of Blade Runner."

## Research

**MiSynth** (2020–present): A music-neurotechnology startup SENAIDA founded as a freshman class project at NYU Clive Davis. MiSynth uses EEG/BCIs to translate brainwave activity into synthesized MIDI, essentially turning the mind into a synthesizer. The concept moved quickly from class project to NYC Media Lab incubator, with neuroscientists and programmers hired for 12 weeks of structured research. SENAIDA received the 2021 NYU Undergraduate Research Fund Award for this work.

**Cyborg Synchrony** (2025): A peer-reviewed paper published in *Frontiers in Computer Science*, co-authored with Jason Snell. The paper proposes an Affective Generative Music-AI system using real-time physiological data (EEG, ECG, GSR, motion) to generate personalized music that promotes physiological synchrony — and therefore social bonding — between users. Unlike existing biofeedback tools focused on individual optimization, the framework targets interpersonal entrainment. SENAIDA has performed techno sets using a Muse biosensor headband, with the heartbeat triggering the kick drum.

**Sonic Cyberfeminism scholarship**: SENAIDA has written academically and publicly about cyberfeminism in electronic music, grounding the work in Haraway's cyborg theory and Legacy Russell's Glitch Feminism. Their Medium essay identifies eight domains for change in music technology, from sonic activism to ethics in AI composition.

**Low Res MFA thesis research (in progress)**: The [[sources/notion-reading-list]] reveals a major active research direction not previously captured elsewhere — a deep literature review into **gesture-controlled music interfaces and embodied music cognition** (NIME: New Interfaces for Musical Expression). The cluster includes 15+ scientific papers on topics such as gesture-to-sound mapping, deformable sensor interfaces, motion-controlled instruments, and the relationship between gesture and musical signification. This is the spine of what appears to be a thesis project. The research sits between [[Concepts/Biofeedback-Music]] (physiological data → music) and [[Concepts/Sonic-Architecture]] (sound as spatial/embodied experience) — the specific angle is *gesture* as the mediating term. The body moves; the sound responds. See [[sources/notion-reading-list]] for the full bibliography.

## Entrepreneurship & community

**WE ARE THE FUTURE!** (2023–present): An underground interdisciplinary collective, label, and curated art party in NYC — a queer BIPOC space focused on bringing together artists and DJs for co-creative sharing. WATF! held a bi-monthly residency at XPIZZA until the venue's 2024 closure. SENAIDA describes this as the project they're most proud of, citing its direct impact on connecting artists who go on to collaborate.

**Flutter** (current): An Apple Watch app for long-distance couples, founded by SENAIDA. As of May 2026, Flutter has approximately 45 users (early adopter stage). A $40k friends-and-family raise is being pursued. The Life Audit recommends repositioning Flutter as the consumer entry point for Cyberian's broader neurofeedback + music wellness platform — rather than leading with the couples haptics concept, which sits in a crowded market.

**Cyberian Technologies** (current): A neuroaesthetics/biofeedback SaaS and luxury wellness startup co-founded with Jason Snell (also SENAIDA's Berlin roommate). SENAIDA serves as CEO. The company's broadest vision — pioneering music and sound healing in the neurowellness and longevity industry — represents a genuinely defensible intersection of SENAIDA's credentials: classical music training, neuroscience research, underground music culture credibility, and deep community networks.

**Youth Showcase Concert Series** (2019–): Founded in Toronto, annual summer music festival for artists under 25. Ran an online version during COVID-19.

**Gender Equality in Music (GEM) Club**: Founded at NYU; grew to 300+ members in its first year. SENAIDA served as president.

**Co-host, "Classy & Sassy"**: A radio show on WNYU 89.1 FM featuring underrepresented contemporary artists.

## Relationship with work

Work is clearly central to identity and not compartmentalized from it. The range is unusually broad — classical training, club culture, academic research, entrepreneurship, visual art — but there is a consistent throughline: the use of technology to create collective, embodied experiences that challenge existing systems and build community for people who have been excluded from those systems.

A complication surfaced in the Life Audit: the relationship to *making* is more complicated than the relationship to *work*. SENAIDA has not made music in 18 months. The career of being an artist is active; the private practice of being an artist is on pause. This is the central creative tension as of May 2026.
